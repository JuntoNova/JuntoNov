A stake in the dream

The American Dream works best when every citizen owns a real stake in the country’s future.

When the costs of housing, education, energy, healthcare, and taxes stay too high, ownership slips out of reach. People stop building and start renting just to survive. Trust fades.

Junto Nova identifies the barriers that keep costs high, turns them into clear investable strategies, and builds the organizations and deals that actually lower them. We prove what works in a few states and then scale it. Our goal is straightforward: make it possible for every American family to buy and own a genuine stake in the dream.

We partner with funders and builders who want to lower the real cost of living so more families can own a stake instead of renting their future.

Why this matters

Who we work with

Funders, builders, and operators who believe high costs of living are not inevitable, and that more competition, better policy, and new supply are the most reliable ways to bring them down so ordinary families can own real stakes.

The name

Junto comes from Benjamin Franklin’s club for mutual improvement: practical people solving real problems together. Nova points to a new start. The work is practical action that expands ownership and lowers costs.

How we think about it

When families can own instead of rent their housing, skills, energy, healthcare, and a larger share of what they earn, their success and the country’s success become the same thing. High explicit taxes, growing public debt, and the quiet tax of inflation all reduce that stake. We focus on the barriers that block ownership and on the deals and organizations that remove them.
