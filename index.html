Let us explain

Who we serve

Cause-motivated philanthropies, education innovators, and business leaders who believe the cost of living in America has become artificially high, and that choice, competition, and new supply are the most powerful levers to bring it down.

What's with the name

Junto refers to Benjamin Franklin's club for mutual improvement: practical people solving real problems together. Nova signals a new beginning and abundance. Together they capture the work: high-leverage, practical action that expands opportunity and lowers costs.

Our operating principle

Education is the highest-leverage starting point. When families have real choice and new high-quality supply enters the market, costs fall and outcomes rise. The same logic applies to housing supply, energy permitting, and healthcare transparency. Lowering the cost of living is not zero-sum; it is engineering win-wins at scale.
