What would I hire you to do?

Junto Nova finds what keeps household costs high and does the work that lowers prices.

Diagnose is public. Cost Drivers is the household bill. A real fight (Social Security now, then housing, school, power, health, taxes) opens a page for that slice: the diagnosis, then the path on that front.

A hire is not a catalog. A hire is a slice on one front. A client can buy one stage or the path. Day one is not a menu.

What a hire on a front can include.

Diagnose
Independent analysis of what actually keeps that slice high.
Strategy
A plan with numbers, targets, and a path to capital or political support.
Capital
Term design and agreements that turn that plan into funded action.
Organization
Stand up the work, run the campaign, move the rule.
Govern
Board seats and oversight after the first wins.

Two ways to hire. Keep the work proprietary. Or let the key insights be published so no one pays to learn the same lessons again. The first pass on a new front costs more. Later work on a similar problem moves faster.
