Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

What this site does

Junto Nova is a strategy consulting firm. This website is primarily informational. We do not run user accounts, online stores, or complex tracking systems.

Information we collect

If you email us (info@juntonova.com) or contact us through other channels, we receive whatever information you choose to include in that message. We use that information only to respond and to manage any resulting professional relationship.

Like most websites, Cloudflare, which hosts and delivers this site, may automatically log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited. We do not use this data to identify individuals for marketing purposes.

How we use information

We use contact information solely to reply to inquiries, schedule conversations, and carry out the work you engage us to do. We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their marketing.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set cookies of its own. The client login page loads Google’s sign-in script, which can set Google’s own cookies. We do not use advertising trackers.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages get read. It does not use cookies and does not follow you across other sites. We do not use it to identify individual visitors.

Pages load fonts, icons and code libraries from outside providers, currently Google Fonts, jsDelivr, cdnjs and the Tailwind CSS content network, and the client login page also loads Google sign-in. When your browser fetches those files, those providers receive your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies.

If we add other analytics, we will update this page first.

Third-party services

Email and any scheduling tools we use are provided by third-party services that have their own privacy practices. We choose providers that treat professional correspondence with appropriate care.

Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us. We keep correspondence and related records only as long as needed for the professional relationship or as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask us to delete it (subject to any legal obligations we may have). Contact us at the email below.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the most recent version.
