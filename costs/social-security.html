Social Security and retirement

Social Security and retirement is 12% of a four-person household bill in 2023, about $14,900 a year. That is the CEX Social Security and retirement line. Payroll is already inside. Life insurance is not.

The 2050 sketch puts this slice at 20% of the bill if household outflows fill Social Security insolvency. Mix shift, not a forecast. Current-law SSA after 2034 is a benefit cut, not this path.

A hire on this front is work that keeps this slice from eating the rest of the household bill.

Stand up the work, run the campaign, move the rule.
